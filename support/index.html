Stock Balloons for iPhone

How can we help?

Questions, feedback, and feature ideas are welcome. If something is not working as expected, send us a note and include the details below.

Email Stock Balloons Support

Contact: support-app@curalytics.com

01

Quotes are not updating

Confirm that your device is online, then reopen Stock Balloons. The app may display a cached quote when Yahoo Finance is unavailable or a recent quote cannot be retrieved.

02

A ticker cannot be found

Check the symbol and exchange format. Availability depends on the symbols and history provided by Yahoo Finance, and some securities may not be supported.

03

My investment return looks wrong

Open the stock, review its purchase price and purchase date, and correct either value if needed. Stock Balloons does not connect to your brokerage, so these values are entered manually.

04

Remove a stock or app data

Remove an individual stock from your watchlist using the app’s controls. To remove all locally stored Stock Balloons data, delete the app from your device.

Reporting a problem

A few details help us investigate.

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • Your Stock Balloons app version
  • The affected ticker symbol, if applicable
  • What you expected and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, provided it contains nothing you consider sensitive

About market information

Market information is provided by Yahoo Finance and may be delayed, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable. Stock Balloons is an informational visualization and does not provide investment advice or execute trades.
